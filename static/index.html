B

Book, inspect, or wait?

A finite-horizon decision model accounts for future observations and changing availability. Every inspection or wait uses one decision step.

Leaving has utility 0.
Booking and leaving end the decision.

H

Follow availability over time

A hidden Markov model updates a hidden free/busy state from a sequence of noisy reports, then predicts the next slot.

Use free, busy or unknown, separated by commas. These are invented reports, not collected occupancy data. The initial prior, reliability and transition settings in this lab are shared by the two lab calculations.

The planner receives this sequence and uses the resource's own prior with fixed 0.80 / 0.35 occupancy transitions. Custom lab prior and transitions are not copied. No reservation is created.

Why this is different from a single Bayesian update

Bayes updates a belief from one report. The HMM also models occupancy changing between reports. A sequence can influence the forecast differently from its last report alone.
